# Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to copy encrypted backups from their own home server to their own Google Drive.

- It can only access files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive (Google's "drive.file" access).
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it has no other users.
- The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's own server.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
